Our Story

Founded in 2008 by lead developer Muhammad Daniyal (MDK), MDK Gallery began as a boutique creative studio with a singular passion: visual storytelling. What started with three designers has evolved into a global studio of creative web developers.

Over the years, we have transitioned into a cutting-edge digital incubator. We embrace 3D WebGL rendering, interactive motion typography, and serverless architectures to produce products serving clients across 15+ countries.
