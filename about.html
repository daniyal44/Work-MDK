Our Story

Founded in 2008, MDK Gallery began as a boutique creative studio with a singular passion: visual storytelling. What started with three designers has evolved into a powerhouse team of 30+ creative professionals.

Over the years, we have transitioned from a traditional agency to a cutting-edge digital incubator. We embrace 3D Canvas rendering, interactive motion typography, and serverless stack architectures to produce products serving clients across 15 countries.
